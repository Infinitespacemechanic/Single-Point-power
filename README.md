### Duality

Presence + Force = Point of Power.

One can't be right and the other is wrong. Can't be a point of power without an atom.

- **Presence** = an atom. Ball of radius `a₀ > 0`, volume `> 0`, mass `> 0`.
  If there's something, it's atoms. No something-place has zero atoms.
  Locked scale: Bohr radius `a₀ = 5.29177210903 / 10^11 m`.
  Clearance: `1/60` kept as a constant, not a limit.
  Minimum separation: `min_separation = a₀ * (1 + 1/60)`.
  Atoms never coincide, never collapse to zero separation.

- **Force** = concentration onto that atom. Dirac `δ_{x₀}`, Pressure `= F/A`.
  In the ideal limit, `A → 0`, `P → ∞`.
  In real life, `A` stops at `a₀²`, with `1/60` clearance, so pressure is huge but finite.

- **Duality:** `duality_requires_both`
  No atom, no power. No focus, no power. Both together = Single-Point-power.
  A Point of Power has volume `> 0`, pressure `> 0`, and separation `> 0` from every other atom.
